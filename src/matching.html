How To Play

Goal: Find all matching pairs on the board

Left Click

Flips Card

Left Click to reveal cards, when two cards are revealed they will be checked to confirm if they are a match. If the two cards do not match they will stay revealed for a short time and then get flipped back over. Keep flippings cards until all matches are revealed in order to win.

To start a new game, simply select the difficulty you would like to play at the top of the screen.

Interesting Facts

  • Simple matching games that we know of today have a big origin point of London in 1899 where the Pelman Institute was founded. Using cards with images on the back, you would flip two over at a time until all matches were found. The Pelmanism system of brain training was advertised to strengthen and develop one's mind similar to how physical training can strengthen your body.
  • One of the oldest known matching games is a Japanese game from the 13th century named Kai-awase or "shell-matching". This game involved elaborately painted shells that were flipped and mixed up where two players would take turns trying to find matching pairs. Gold and Silver were eventually added to the shells making them become more of a decorative item and in turn becoming a popular blessing in upper-class weddings.
