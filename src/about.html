A Little Bit About Me

Interests

Sports

Watching sports is something I have loved for a long time. The main sport of choice is American Football with my favorite team being the New York Giants. Being a fan of the Giants comes with an interesting challenge when one of your best friends happens to be a Commanders fan. With both teams being rivals in the same division we love a good bit of trash talk during football season. We make a point to try to attend the Giants vs Commanders game in DC every year so long as scheduling works out. Watching on TV is fun but nothing compares to the atmosphere of a live stadium full of fans. My bucket list includes watching a game in every stadium in the US with the hopes of attending some international games.

In May of 2025 I found myself particularly bored one Sunday flipping between streaming services trying to just kill some time. I happened to click into one that just so happened to be streaming an F1 race in Miami and my only thought was, "why not?". From then on I was completely hooked. Despite never watching any form of racing I have since seen every F1 race and even dived a bit into the archives. No allegiance to any one team but have a small list of favorite drivers that I root for on race day. As most races occur overseas the majority of them take place early in the morning on Sunday, it has become a tradition of mine to wake up even earlier in order to pick up something special for breakfast to have while watching the race.

Video Games

My interest for Gaming has changed a lot of over the years but it is one thing that has always been a part of my life. As a young kid I estimate a couple thousand hours were put into my PlayStation 2 by itself. My all time favorite game series is Ratchet & Clank, so much so that my cat is actually named Ratchet after the main protagonist. In recent time I have noticed myself switching over from competitive first person shooters to slower paced cozy games but the range is still there. I love playing an extraction shooter like Arc Raiders with friends and then having some me time putting the fine touches on my Stardew Valley farm.

Dungeons & Dragons

I began playing Dungeons & Dragons a few years ago with a group of friends solely because they needed an extra person. At first I thought it would be this kind of silly thing that we would try and then move on from rather quickly. Well I was definitely wrong about that. Theres nothing like being a flying bird person archer wearing a top hat fighting a sentient broom to help fuel your imagination. Picking up Bulder's Gate 3 on Xbox was a surprisingly good way to help with learning a lot of the mechanics of the game while also giving a lot of wonderful ideas on how to interpret the D&D world. One of these days I would like to finally jot down all of my own ideas and finally build out a custom homebrew game.
